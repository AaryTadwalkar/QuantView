## 0. Hard-Blocker Enforcement Rule (`UNVERIFIED_BLOCKER`, `HLD §3.2`, `LLD §0`)

Per Audit Blocker 3, any item in Section 1 (`VER-01..VER-06`) or Section 2 (`S1..S12`) with status `UNVERIFIED_BLOCKER` is a **hard prerequisite gate** for the implementation tickets listed in its **"Blocked Dependent Tickets"** column:
- No coding agent or engineer may mark a blocked dependent ticket `IMPLEMENTED`, `TESTED`, or `VERIFIED` while its prerequisite `VER-*` or `S*` item remains `UNVERIFIED_BLOCKER`.
- Clearing an `UNVERIFIED_BLOCKER` requires recording in `config/rules/nifty_rules.yaml`, `config/cost_schedules.yaml`, or `config/data_semantics.yaml`: (a) the verified parameter value, (b) the authoritative primary-source URL, circular number, or empirical test artifact path, (c) the effective date (`YYYY-MM-DD`), and (d) the verification timestamp.
