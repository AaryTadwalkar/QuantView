## 2. Deliverables Summary

1. **`services/collector_ws/framing.py`:**
   - Implements `pack_frame()`, `unpack_frames_from_bytes()`, and `ParsedFrame`.
   - Framing format: `[QVWS: 4B][SeqNum: 8B][RecvTimeUs: 8B][PayloadLen: 4B][CRC32: 4B][Payload: N bytes]`.
   - Defensive stream unpacker cleanly separates intact frames from truncated trailing fragments.

2. **`services/collector_ws/raw_segment_writer.py`:**
   - Implements `RawSegmentWriter` and `SegmentManifest`.
   - Handles live streaming to `.bin.partial` files with batched fsync (default 1.0s interval).
   - Atomic segment finalization: compresses to `.bin.gz`, computes SHA-256 digests, publishes `.manifest.json`.
   - Implements automated crash recovery `recover_session_partials()` and forensic auditing `reconcile_session_directory()`.

3. **`services/collector_ws/__init__.py`:**
   - Re-exports `RawSegmentWriter`, `SegmentManifest`, `ParsedFrame`, `pack_frame`, and `unpack_frames_from_bytes`.

4. **`tests/unit/test_collector_ws.py`:**
   - 9-test suite verifying binary protocol round-trips, batched fsync, rotation, crash kill simulation, truncated tail quarantine, and tamper detection.
