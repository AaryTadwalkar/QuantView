## 2. Mathematical Definition and Confirmation Timing

### 2.1 Swing Pivot Formulation
Given series highs $H$ and lows $L$, with left lookback window $W_L = 5$ and right confirmation window $W_R = 5$:
- **Swing High at bar $i$:**
  $$\forall l \in [1, W_L]: H[i] > H[i - l] \quad \land \quad \forall r \in [1, W_R]: H[i] \ge H[i + r]$$
- **Swing Low at bar $i$:**
  $$\forall l \in [1, W_L]: L[i] < L[i - l] \quad \land \quad \forall r \in [1, W_R]: L[i] \le L[i + r]$$

### 2.2 Point-in-Time Availability Rule
The pivot at bar $i$ cannot be known at bar $i$. It becomes causally confirmed only upon the close of bar $t = i + W_R$:
$$\text{confirmed\_at}(i) = \text{ts\_close}(i + W_R)$$
Prior to bar $i + W_R$, `last_confirmed_high` and `last_confirmed_low` retain their previous confirmed levels or `None`.

## 3. Classification State Machine & CHoCH Transition Matrix

QuantView implements the canonical 6-row transition matrix specified in `ENGINE §21.2`:

| Current Market Bias | Penetrated Level | Classified Event | Resulting Market Bias | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `NEUTRAL` | Confirmed Swing High | `BULLISH_CHOCH` | `BULLISH` | Initial trend establishment |
| `NEUTRAL` | Confirmed Swing Low | `BEARISH_CHOCH` | `BEARISH` | Initial trend establishment |
| `BULLISH` | Confirmed Swing High | `BULLISH_BOS` | `BULLISH` | Trend continuation |
| `BULLISH` | Confirmed Swing Low | `BEARISH_CHOCH` | `BEARISH` | Trend reversal |
| `BEARISH` | Confirmed Swing Low | `BEARISH_BOS` | `BEARISH` | Trend continuation |
| `BEARISH` | Confirmed Swing High | `BULLISH_CHOCH` | `BULLISH` | Trend reversal |
