## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone **M2-05** implements the Upstox V3 Market Data Feed WebSocket client connection lifecycle (`UpstoxWebSocketClient`) and versioned Google Protocol Buffers decoder (`WsQuoteDecoder`), converting raw binary WebSocket streaming frames into canonical `WsQuote` models conforming strictly to `QUANTVIEW_DATA_SPEC §4.5`.

In alignment with the Stage 2 implementation plan safeguards and `QUANTVIEW_MASTER_SPEC §3`:
1. **Raw-First Persistence Invariant:** Incoming binary frames are written to `.bin.partial` files with batched `os.fsync()` (crash loss window $\le 1\text{ s}$) before any decoding or downstream distribution occurs.
2. **Decoder Scaffolding & Safeguard:** The decoder is scaffolded against the official Upstox V3 Protobuf definitions (`MarketDataFeedV3.proto`) and verified offline against synthetic unit fixtures. In compliance with the specification safeguard, quotes decoded prior to live market session recordings are explicitly tagged with `SCAFFOLDED_DECODER_UNVERIFIED_FIXTURE` in `quality_flags`. Full production certification is deferred until two representative live market session recordings are captured.
3. **Fail-Safe Decoder Isolation:** Malformed or corrupted binary payloads are safely caught without crashing the collector process or dropping raw bytes.
4. **Data Quality Flags:** Zero bid/ask depth is flagged as `ZERO_DEPTH`. Open interest on non-derivative instruments (e.g., indices or equities) is flagged as `NON_OPTION_OI`.

## 4. Operational Readiness & Operator CLI Tool

The standalone operational CLI tool is published at:
[`tools/ops/run_collector_ws.py`](file:///c:/Algo/quantview/tools/ops/run_collector_ws.py)

**Usage Command:**
```bash
python tools/ops/run_collector_ws.py --instruments "NSE_INDEX|Nifty 50" --mode full
```

**Features Provided:**
- Native token readiness check via `TokenManager` (exits 1 if token expired or missing).
- Kernel-level single owner lease acquisition (`SingleOwnerLease`).
- Real-time streaming into `.bin.partial` raw segments with batched `fsync`.
- Periodic atomic dead-man heartbeats written to `Data/raw/telemetry/heartbeat_collector_ws.json`.
- Clean shutdown on `Ctrl+C` (SIGINT/SIGTERM) with buffer rotation and lock removal.
