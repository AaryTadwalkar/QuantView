## 2. Fundamental Balance Invariants

At any point in time $t$:
$$\text{Total Equity}_t = \text{Cash Balance}_t + \text{Long Position Market Value}_t$$
$$\text{Total Equity}_t = \text{Initial Capital} + \text{Net Realized P\&L}_t + \text{Unrealized P\&L}_t$$
$$\text{Net Realized P\&L}_t = \text{Gross Realized P\&L}_t - \text{Total Fees Paid}_t$$
