## 4. Remaining Limitations & Operating Scope Boundaries

1. **Short Options in `REALISTIC_ACCOUNT` Mode:**
   - Real-time exchange clearing SPAN risk parameter files (`.spn` and `.spd`) are absent.
   - Any attempt to run short options or spreads with short legs under `REALISTIC_ACCOUNT` strictly raises `RealisticMarginBlockedError: MARGIN_NOT_VERIFIED`.
   - Short option execution is verified exclusively under explicitly labelled `MarginMode.THEORETICAL`.
2. **Expiry Cash Settlement:**
   - Formal 15:30 closing VWAP cash settlement is not yet modeled as an automated engine event.
   - All open backtesting positions are subject to mandatory 15:25 IST force-flat square-off (`FORCE_FLAT_1525`).
3. **Execution Size:**
   - Partial fills are deferred per `ENGINE §24`; orders exceeding bar candle volume emit `VOLUME_EXCEEDED_WARNING` and fill atomically at the next-bar open.
