## 2. Deliverables Summary

1. **`packages/data/budget.py`:**
   - Implements `RateBudgetManager`, `BudgetConfig`, `RequestPriority`, and `BudgetExhaustedError`.
   - Thread-safe sliding window tracking (1-second and 60-second deques) with microsecond precision.
   - Exchange calendar market-hours detection (`09:15:00` to `15:30:00` in `Asia/Kolkata` on Monday–Friday).
   - Priority-tier scheduling: `HIGH` (live snapshots) vs `LOW` (historical backfill).
   - Pause/throttle interface: `can_backfill_proceed()`, `acquire()`, `notify_rate_limit_hit()`, `get_status()`.

2. **`packages/data/__init__.py`:**
   - Re-exports `RateBudgetManager`, `BudgetConfig`, `RequestPriority`, and `BudgetExhaustedError` for clean consumption across packages and services without broker SDK dependencies.

3. **`services/collector_snapshot/adapter.py`:**
   - Integrated with `RateBudgetManager`: acquires high-priority budget before query and parses `Retry-After` header on 429 to trigger backfill cooldown.

4. **`tests/unit/test_rate_budget.py`:**
   - 10-test suite verifying market hours boundaries, priority reservation, utilization thresholds, 429 cooldowns, timeout errors, and adapter callbacks.
