## 2. Fundamental Accounting Equations & Invariants

To avoid double-counting fees or mislabeling P&L, all scenarios and projection logic strictly enforce:

1. **Fundamental Balance Equation (Evaluated after EVERY event):**
   $$\text{Cash Balance}_t + \text{Long Position Market Value}_t = \text{Initial Capital} + \text{Gross Realized P\&L}_t + \text{Unrealized P\&L}_t - \text{Total Fees Paid}_t$$
2. **Net Realized P&L Definition:**
   $$\text{Net Realized P\&L}_t = \text{Gross Realized P\&L}_t - \text{Total Fees Paid}_t$$
3. **Total Account Equity Definition:**
   $$\text{Total Equity}_t = \text{Cash Balance}_t + \text{Long Position Market Value}_t = \text{Initial Capital} + \text{Net Realized P\&L}_t + \text{Unrealized P\&L}_t$$
4. **Final-Bar Rule Invariant:**
   A strategy signal decided on the completed 15:29:00 bar has no subsequent regular-session bar; it **cannot fill at 15:29:00 close** and is rejected with `NO_NEXT_BAR`. Pre-scheduled liquidations (force-flat) that entered the final bar still open execute at 15:29:00 close with `EOD_CLOSE_FILL`.
