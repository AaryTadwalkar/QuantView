## 2. Empirical Measurement Methodology & Findings

Measurements were conducted directly on the 5 finalized compressed segments (`Data/raw/upstox_ws/ticks/trade_date=2026-10-26/`) and 86 full option chain snapshots (`Data/raw/upstox_rest/snapshots/trade_date=2026-10-26/`):

1. **Throughput & Frame Rates:**
   - **Total Recorded Frames:** `33,991 frames` (containing 134,003 quote updates across Nifty 50 and 22 option contracts).
   - **P50 Rate:** 7 ticks/second.
   - **P95 Rate:** 7 ticks/second.
   - **Peak Burst Rate:** 12 ticks/second.
2. **Compression Efficiency:**
   - **Uncompressed Wire Size:** 44.36 MB (46,514,723 bytes).
   - **Finalized Compressed Size:** 6.47 MB (6,781,612 bytes).
   - **Overall Compression Ratio:** **6.86:1 (85.4% disk space savings)**.
   - **Average Uncompressed Frame:** 1,368.4 bytes.
   - **Average Compressed Frame:** 199.5 bytes.
3. **Snapshot Footprint:**
   - **Captures Recorded:** 86 snapshots.
   - **Average Compressed Snapshot:** 16,678 bytes (144 strikes $\times$ 2 contracts).
   - **Total Daily Snapshot Storage:** ~1.45 MB.

## 4. Hardware Sizing Evidence

- A **512 GB SSD** provides over **2.5+ years of raw unpruned retention** for both tick-by-tick and full option chain snapshots.
- A **1 TB SSD** provides over **5+ years of retention**.
