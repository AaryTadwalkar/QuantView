## 2. Mathematical Slippage Formulation

Given raw execution price $P_{\text{raw}}$, slippage $S$ in paise, and tick size $T$ in paise:
- **BUY:**
  $$P_{\text{slipped}} = P_{\text{raw}} + S$$
  $$P_{\text{fill}} = \left\lfloor \frac{P_{\text{slipped}} + T - 1}{T} \right\rfloor \times T$$
- **SELL:**
  $$P_{\text{slipped}} = P_{\text{raw}} - S$$
  $$P_{\text{fill}} = \left\lfloor \frac{P_{\text{slipped}}}{T} \right\rfloor \times T$$
