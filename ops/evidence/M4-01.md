## 2. Mathematical & Property Test Invariants

### 2.1 Exact Money Conversion Rules
```python
to_paise(Decimal("150.25")) -> Paise(15025)
to_paise("150.25")          -> Paise(15025)
to_paise(150)               -> Paise(15000)
to_paise(150.25)            -> Paise(15025)
to_paise(150.2578)          -> raises PriceUnquantizableError(code="PRICE_NOT_QUANTIZABLE")
```

### 2.2 Tick Quantization Invariants (ENGINE §4 Step 1)
Given price $P$ in integer paise and tick size $T$ in integer paise:
- **For BUY:**
  $$P_{\text{quantized}} = \left\lfloor \frac{P + T - 1}{T} \right\rfloor \times T$$
  Guarantees: $P_{\text{quantized}} \ge P$, $P_{\text{quantized}} < P + T$, and $P_{\text{quantized}} \pmod T = 0$.
- **For SELL:**
  $$P_{\text{quantized}} = \left\lfloor \frac{P}{T} \right\rfloor \times T$$
  Guarantees: $P_{\text{quantized}} \le P$, $P_{\text{quantized}} > P - T$, and $P_{\text{quantized}} \pmod T = 0$.
