## 2. Mathematical Definition

European index options on NSE (NIFTY) are evaluated under the forward Black-76 formulation:
$$d_1 = \frac{\ln(F / K) + \frac{1}{2} \sigma^2 \tau}{\sigma \sqrt{\tau}}, \quad d_2 = d_1 - \sigma \sqrt{\tau}$$
$$\text{Call} = e^{-r \tau} [F N(d_1) - K N(d_2)], \quad \text{Put} = e^{-r \tau} [K N(-d_2) - F N(-d_1)]$$

Analytical Greeks derivatives:
- **Delta ($\Delta$):** $\Delta_{\text{Call}} = e^{-r \tau} N(d_1) \in [0, 1]$, $\Delta_{\text{Put}} = -e^{-r \tau} N(-d_1) \in [-1, 0]$
- **Gamma ($\Gamma$):** $\Gamma = \frac{e^{-r \tau} N'(d_1)}{F \sigma \sqrt{\tau}}$ ($1/\text{INR}$)
- **Vega ($\mathcal{V}$):** $\mathcal{V} = 0.01 \times F e^{-r \tau} N'(d_1) \sqrt{\tau}$ ($\text{INR} / 1\%\text{ IV}$)
- **Theta ($\Theta$):** 1 calendar day decay ($\Delta t = 1/365\text{ year}$)
